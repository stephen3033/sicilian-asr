# Italian pronunciation and vocabulary repair

> Historical evidence from 2026-10-08. Commands and paths below describe that checkout; see [current validation](../validation.md) for the layer refactor.

Validated on 2026-10-08 using the existing ZIPA checkpoint, CPU backend, Compact CTC topology, PhonItalia dictionary, and MLS trigram. The default Italian configuration now enables audited pronunciation alternatives and contraction derivation. Acoustic/LM scales remain 1/1; the vocabulary limit remains 20,000.

## Outcome

The repair passed the predeclared adoption gate: lower spoken-form WER without worse spoken-form CER on both development and held-out test recordings. On the held-out set, spoken-form word errors fell from **369 to 204**, a **44.72% relative reduction**. Strict word errors fell from **383 to 218**.

| Split / configuration | Recordings | Strict WER | Strict CER | Spoken-form WER | Spoken-form CER |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Development, original | 165 | 34.14% | 13.70% | 33.13% | 12.89% |
| Development, repaired | 165 | 22.02% | 8.87% | 20.91% | 8.19% |
| Held-out test, original | 202 | 30.69% | 11.45% | 29.57% | 10.58% |
| Held-out test, repaired | 202 | 17.47% | 6.73% | 16.35% | 6.03% |

Development has 990 reference words; test has 1,248. These are aggregate edit counts divided by aggregate reference lengths, not averages of per-recording percentages. Strict character denominators are 4,388/5,513; spoken-form denominators are 4,430/5,559.

The original acceptance clip is excluded from test. Its separate end-to-end MP4 rerun now produces:

```text
Publisher reference: L’ho incontrato oggi dopo 2 anni
Old output:          lo ha incontrato dogi dopo due anni
Repaired output:     l'ho incontrato oggi dopo due anni
Chosen pronunciation: ▁lɔinkontratoɔddʒidopodueanni
```

Its strict WER is **16.67% (1/6)** and strict CER **11.11% (3/27)**, entirely due to `2` versus `due`. Spoken-form WER/CER are both **0%**. The rerun exercised FFmpeg extraction, ZIPA inference, the full prepared graph, evaluation, probability export, and candidate diagnostics. It took 33.95 seconds including verification/loading and peaked at 9.50 GB process RSS. The original acceptance artifacts remain byte-for-byte unchanged against their recorded hashes.

## What caused the errors

The source dictionary required `ɔdʒːi` for `oggi`, `anːi` for `anni`, and `iŋkontrato` for `incontrato`; the acoustic output favored `ɔddʒi`, `anni`, and `inkontrato`. Token representability alone had not established compatibility with the model's learned transcription conventions. The dictionary also lacked internal-apostrophe words entirely, although MLS contains common contractions such as `l'ho`.

The initial controlled candidate comparison found that the original graph narrowly preferred `dogi` over `oggi`: its acoustic advantage outweighed the LM's preference for `oggi` by only 0.12 natural-log score units. Using doubled-consonant pronunciations reversed that comparison by 7.14 units. Wider search did not fix the original output. WAV and MP4 greedy IPA agreed, while their erroneous word outputs differed, demonstrating fragile word decoding.

Development ablations confirm that pronunciation compatibility accounts for the largest measured improvement:

| Configuration, acoustic scale 1 | Spoken-form WER | Spoken-form CER |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Original graph | 33.13% | 12.89% |
| Pronunciation alternatives only | 22.83% | 8.51% |
| Contractions only | 31.52% | 12.60% |
| Both changes | 20.91% | 8.19% |

Both changes preserve source pronunciations and original retained LM weights. Alternatives include doubled consonants, doubled affricate closure, and prevelar `ŋ → n`. Contractions are derived from independently attested MLS `l'`/`c'` spellings and existing vowel-initial suffix pronunciations, before frequency-based vocabulary selection. No reference words are injected into preparation. See [the conversion rules](../linguistic-conversion.md).

## Selection and validation

The study used all neutral recordings from Emozionalmente's publisher development and test splits: 54 development speakers and 59 test speakers, with no overlap between these splits. The previously inspected `1612969363951.wav` was excluded from the test set. Publisher text remained unchanged, including digit and `E’` spellings. No transcripts were corrected using model output.

The 367 original WAV files were inferred once. All graph and scale comparisons reused identical saved probability matrices. Temporary pronunciation-only and contractions-only graphs were removed after their development evaluations; their receipts and results were retained. The original and combined graphs remain available.

The combined graph was evaluated at all five planned acoustic scales:

| Acoustic scale | Development spoken-form WER | Development spoken-form CER |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 0.50 | 33.23% | 18.87% |
| 0.75 | 22.12% | 10.09% |
| **1.00** | **20.91%** | **8.19%** |
| 1.50 | 22.02% | 6.12% |
| 2.00 | 24.75% | 5.89% |

Selection minimized development spoken-form WER, with CER and then distance from scale 1 as tie breakers. The winner was written to `selection.json` before either held-out decoding run. Larger scales improved character accuracy but increased word errors; the WER-first criterion retained scale 1. No held-out results were used for tuning.

- **71 tests passed**, including affricates/geminates, nasal alternatives, LM-attested contractions, conservative Italian number verbalization, strict metric preservation, aggregate scoring, cache identity, and diagnostic score checks against KenLM.
- Strict resource validation reports Italian resources and the prepared decoder ready.
- The separate MP4 acceptance succeeds with `--diagnostics`, including original probability export and competing sampled paths.
- Existing source dictionary, ARPA, checkpoint, and original acceptance artifacts were preserved.

## Interfaces and artifacts

`decoder.pronunciation_variants` and `decoder.contractions` are boolean graph settings and participate in cache identity. Original configuration files without these options retain the previous lexical behavior; the supplied `configs/ita.yaml` now enables both. Re-run preparation when changing either option.

Existing top-level `wer` and `cer` retain their strict meaning. The additive `spoken_form` object includes separate scores, alignments, OOV counts, and per-side normalization transformations. Standalone integer verbalization covers 0–999999; ambiguous numeric forms are left unconverted. Contractions, grammatical differences, and accent differences remain scored.

`transcribe --diagnostics` also exports original posteriors and `diagnostics.json`. Each segment includes chosen pronunciation and scaled acoustic/grammar score contributions. Candidate diagnostics include the actual best path and distinct sentences sampled from the lattice. These samples are not exhaustive N-best scores or calibrated confidence estimates, and references never propose candidates.

Primary artifacts, relative to the repository root:

- `outputs/ita_repair_study/report.json`: adoption gate and aggregate before/after results.
- `outputs/ita_repair_study/study.json`, `selection.json`, and `frozen-config.yaml`: input/code provenance, speaker/recording selection, and frozen development choice.
- `outputs/ita_repair_study/results/`: per-recording JSONL plus aggregate, per-sentence, and per-speaker summaries for every evaluated configuration.
- `outputs/ita_repair_study/probabilities/`: identical acoustic evidence reused by the comparisons, with hashes in `probabilities_manifest.json`.
- `outputs/ita_acceptance_repaired/`: the new MP4 run, metrics, probabilities, and candidate diagnostics.
- `.cache/graphs/ita/f7cce041b64b63d2af5fd2e1b65ab5f6b2c6048d6de4a51e9cacdbfc066dba20/`: validated combined graph and pronunciation audits.

## Remaining limits before SCN

This is a regression corpus of **18 repeated, acted sentences**, with speaker-disjoint development/test partitions. It does not establish accuracy on broad Italian conversation, long recordings, or Sicilian. The repairs improve the shared decoding foundation; they do not validate an SCN pronunciation inventory or language model.

Remaining test errors include **28 `e → è` substitutions** caused by publisher `E’` spellings passing through the strict normalizer. These remain visible rather than being silently corrected. The selected vocabulary still excludes `basket`, `stellata`, `telefonico`, and `verifica`: **32 spoken-form reference tokens (2.56%)**. Other errors include word boundaries, auxiliary deletion, and grammatical/vowel choices. Consequently, the remaining 16.35% spoken-form WER is not purely an acoustic-model error rate.

The combined full lexicon has 100,343 words, including 6,597 derived spellings and 51,021 added pronunciation pairs. The shared LM vocabulary grows to 78,883; 641 derived spellings enter the fixed 20,000-word selection. The final graph grows from 4.51 GB to **6.22 GB**, with 109,100,799 states and 254,250,554 arcs. Larger vocabulary coverage and additional pronunciation alternatives have material memory/storage costs.
