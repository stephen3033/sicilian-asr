# DOS24 references

The DOS24 v2.2 and 2017 PDFs are acquired unchanged under `.cache/scn/acquired/`; their source-specific terms, hashes and dates are recorded in the [catalog](../sources/catalog.json). `compatibility.json` records the comparison and the absence of an automatic spelling conversion.

See [the orthographic analysis](../../../docs/scn-orthography-analysis.md) and the
[expanded DOS24 audit](../../../docs/scn-dos24-audit.md). Versioned rules and
contextual review decisions are in `resources/scn/audit/`; verified material,
supported/inferred material and the unreviewed pool are reported separately.
